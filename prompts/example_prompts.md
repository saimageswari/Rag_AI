# Example Prompts

Use these as quick starts in the chat input or as inspiration for suggested questions.

## Summarization
- Summarize this document in 10 bullet points.
- What are the key decisions, risks, and next steps?
- Explain the executive summary in plain English.

## Extraction
- List all names, organizations, projects, dates, amounts, and metrics.
- Extract all action items and owners.
- Create a table of the most important facts in this document.

## Analysis
- What are the strongest arguments in this document?
- What assumptions does the document make?
- What information is missing or unclear?

## Learning
- Generate 10 multiple-choice questions with answers.
- Explain the hardest section using simple examples.
- Create a study guide from this document.

## Data Files
- Summarize the rows and columns in this CSV or spreadsheet.
- What are the outliers or unusual values?
- What trends are visible from the data?
